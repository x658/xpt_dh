# Privacy Policy

At **XPT Dev** (`https://xptdev.com`), accessible from `https://xptdev.com`, one of our main priorities is the privacy and security of our visitors and developer community. This Privacy Policy document details the types of information that are collected and recorded by XPT Dev and how we use and safeguard that information.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at **admin@xptdev.com**.

---

## 1. Zero-Knowledge Client-Side Developer Utilities

XPT Dev provides a suite of online developer utilities (`/tools`), including JSON formatting and validation, Base64/URL encoding, UUID v4 generation, SHA-256/SHA-512 cryptographic hashing, Unix timestamp conversion, and high-entropy secret generation.

* **100% Local Browser Execution**: All inputs, payloads, tokens, code snippets, and cryptographic strings entered into our developer tools are processed strictly inside your browser's local sandbox using native **Web Cryptography APIs** and JavaScript engines.
* **No Server Transmission or Storage**: XPT Dev **never** transmits, logs, stores, or inspects the payloads you process within our client-side utilities. Once you close or refresh your browser tab, your input data is permanently cleared from browser volatile memory.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Voluntarily Provided Information
When you voluntarily subscribe to our engineering newsletter or contact us directly via email (`admin@xptdev.com`), we may collect:
* Your email address (for newsletter delivery or responding to your inquiry).
* Any message content or feedback you choose to provide.

You may unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the unsubscribe link included in every email.

### 2.2 Log Files and Analytical Data
Like standard web servers and edge delivery networks (Vercel and Cloudflare), XPT Dev follows a standard procedure of using log files. The information collected by log files includes:
* Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized where applicable)
* Browser type and version
* Internet Service Provider (ISP)
* Date and time stamp
* Referring/exit pages and click counts

These data points are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. Their purpose is to analyze traffic trends, maintain edge security, prevent DDoS abuse, and diagnose technical performance issues.

## 3. Cookies, Google AdSense, and DoubleClick DART Cookie

### 3.1 How We Use Cookies
XPT Dev uses essential cookies and local storage (`localStorage`) to store your interface preferences, such as your selected language locale (`en`, `zh`, `ja`) and visual theme (`light` or `dark` mode).

### 3.2 Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies
We use **Google AdSense** to display advertisements on `https://xptdev.com` to support the maintenance of our free developer tools and technical documentation:
* **Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.**
* **Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve advertisements to our users based on their visit to `https://xptdev.com` and/or other sites on the Internet.**
* **Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising**: Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting **[Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/)** (`https://www.google.com/settings/ads`). Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting **[www.aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/)** or **[www.youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/)**.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our site, please visit Google's official policy page: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 5. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

### 5.1 GDPR Data Protection Rights (EEA Users)
If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following data protection rights:
* **The right to access, update, or delete** the personal information we hold on you.
* **The right of rectification** if your information is inaccurate or incomplete.
* **The right to object** to our processing of your personal data.
* **The right of restriction** and **data portability**.
* **The right to withdraw consent** at any time where we relied on your consent to process your personal information.

### 5.2 CCPA Privacy Rights (California Users)
Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to:
* Request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected about consumers.
* Request that a business delete any personal data about the consumer that a business has collected.
* Request that a business that sells a consumer's personal data, not sell the consumer's personal data (Note: XPT Dev does not sell personal data).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at **admin@xptdev.com**.
