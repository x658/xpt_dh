# About XPT Dev

**XPT Dev** is an open, privacy-first technology platform dedicated to providing free online developer utilities, technical guides, and modern cloud deployment practices.

Our mission is to help software engineers, indie hackers, and founders build and ship digital products faster without privacy trade-offs or costly recurring subscriptions.

---

## 🚀 What We Offer

* **[Online Developer Toolbox](https://tools.xptdev.com)**: Over 100+ lightweight, client-side utilities including JSON formatters, JWT analyzers, hash generators, Base64 converters, and regex sandboxes that execute 100% locally in your browser.
* **[XPT Mail](https://mail.xptdev.com)**: A high-reliability, zero-server-cost private domain email infrastructure built on serverless edge computing.
* **[Technical Blog](/blog)**: Curated architectural walkthroughs, DevOps tutorials, and web development security guides.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Data Sovereignty

We believe your code, tokens, and data belong exclusively to you. 
* None of our client-side developer utilities transmit input payloads to remote backends.
* Cryptographic calculations and token decoding are computed strictly using your local browser engine.
