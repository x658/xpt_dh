In the era of modern personal branding and indie hacking, sending outreach emails from a generic `@gmail.com` or `@outlook.com` address lacks professionalism. However, commercial business email suites (like Google Workspace or Microsoft 365) cost $6 to $18 per user per month.

For developers and founders managing multiple domains, this cost quickly escalates.

Fortunately, by leveraging **Cloudflare Email Routing, Cloudflare Workers, and D1 storage**, you can build a 100% free, production-ready private domain email platform. You can test a live implementation of this system on our dedicated [XPT Mail Service](https://mail.xptdev.com).

---

## Why Serverless Email Infrastructure?

Traditional mail servers require complex configurations: postfix, dovecot, spam filtering, IP reputation warming, and constant patching.

Cloudflare handles the heavy lifting:
1. **Global Anycast Network**: High reliability and built-in DDoS resistance.
2. **Zero Maintenance**: Automated MX routing and zero-cost serverless execution.
3. **Storage Flexibility**: Store received emails in edge databases (Cloudflare D1) or key-value storage (KV).

---

## Step 1: DNS & MX Records Setup

To receive emails on your domain (e.g., `xptdev.com`), you must configure the following Cloudflare Email Routing MX records:

| Record Type | Host | Points To | Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MX** | `@` | `route1.mx.cloudflare.net` | 10 |
| **MX** | `@` | `route2.mx.cloudflare.net` | 73 |
| **MX** | `@` | `route3.mx.cloudflare.net` | 68 |
